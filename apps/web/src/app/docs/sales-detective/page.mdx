# Sales Detective

The Detective builds your **product profile** — the single document every
downstream generator trusts. It has two modes; use both.

## Dump mode

Paste text or point it at a URL. Good raw material, in rough order of value:

- Existing sales pages or VSL scripts (yours or a predecessor's)
- Customer emails, reviews, testimonials — verbatim, unedited
- Support tickets and refund reasons (objections live here)
- Founder rants: why the product exists, who it is NOT for
- Spec sheets, pricing, guarantee terms

The extractor merges each dump into the current profile version. Nothing is
overwritten silently — versions are kept.

## Interrogation mode

After a dump, the Detective lists what it still does not know and asks. The
questions are ordered by how much each answer changes the copy. Answer
conversationally; contract validation happens behind the scenes.

## What "done" means

The checklist marks this step complete when the profile has a product name and
enough substance for the Offer Forge. More material is always better — VOC
(voice of customer) phrases you feed here surface verbatim in copy later,
and the invented-numbers guard means **generators can only use specifics that
exist in your material**.
