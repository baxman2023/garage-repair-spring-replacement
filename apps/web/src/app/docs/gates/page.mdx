# The gate ladder

No asset reaches you without earning it. Gates are not suggestions — a failing
gate blocks the asset and says why.

| Gate | Question it asks |
| ---- | ---------------- |
| G0 | Is the offer approved? |
| G1 | Does the funnel math work at your price and CPCs? |
| G2 | Is the five-market strategy approved? |
| G3 | Does the Council of Copywriters score it ≥ 80 with no lens below 70? |
| G4 | Do simulated cold-traffic personas reach the CTA without bouncing? |
| G5 | Is it free of AI tells, in the right grade band, specific, and rhythmic? |
| G6 | Does it clear the compliance packs for your category? |
| G7 | Does the packaged build kit validate end-to-end? |

## The Council (G3)

Six lenses — Schwartz, Halbert, Bencivenga, Sugarman, Kennedy, Carlton — each
score independently through their own doctrine. Failures come back as ranked
fixes and the asset revises itself before re-submission; persistent failures
escalate to you with the Council's notes.

## The focus group (G4)

Twenty synthetic cold-traffic personas from YOUR diagnosed market read the
asset. Attention drops, disbelief on specific claims, and bounce reasons are
annotated per block; fixes target only the failing blocks.

## De-slop (G5)

Readability band, AI-tell scan, specificity, sentence rhythm, and voice match.
The rewrite loop may only use specifics that exist in your source material.

## Compliance (G6)

Deterministic regex packs per category plus claims cross-checking. **This is an
assistive tool, not legal advice** — the disclaimer on every G6 report means
exactly that. Your compliance obligations remain your own.

Overrides exist for G4 and G6 (with an audit trail) — the machine never
overrides itself.
